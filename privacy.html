Privacy Policy — HumanoidVerse

Effective date: [ADD DATE]

HumanoidVerse ("the app") is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking. There are no user accounts.

Favorites

Robots you mark as favorites are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to us or anyone else, and they are removed if you delete the app.

Network requests

To show robot data and news, the app fetches publicly available content (the project's robot database and a public news feed) over a secure connection. These standard requests are not used to identify or track you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
